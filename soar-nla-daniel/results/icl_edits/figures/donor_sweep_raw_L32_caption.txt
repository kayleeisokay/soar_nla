Donor patching on Gemma-3-12B raw-text ICL prompts without a chat template. Each curve shows the target answer rate after replacing the indicated positions with donor activations at each sampled layer. Results are averaged over 6 tasks, 10 items per task and 3 seeds (180 trials per layer and span). The vertical line marks layer 32, used in the NLA experiments. These sweep results use 3 seeds, whereas the layer-32 edit comparison uses 10.
