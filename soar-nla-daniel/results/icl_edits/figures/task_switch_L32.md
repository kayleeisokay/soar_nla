For task switching, combining answer and task substitutions with a final-paragraph rewrite gives higher average target rates with the chat template than in raw text. Across French-to-Spanish translation, capital-to-currency, and antonym-to-comparative switches, answer-only or task-only edits give average target rates no higher than 0.057. Editing both the answer and task words reaches 0.440 with single-token patching and 0.470 with four-token patching in raw text, compared with 0.463 and 0.543 with the chat template. Adding the final-paragraph rewrite raises the raw-text rates to 0.847 and 0.877, and the chat-template rates to 0.950 and 0.983. With four-token patching, combined edits approach the donor rate in raw text (0.897) and exceed it with the chat template (0.783). Round-trip and no-op controls produce no target answers across 300 trials per condition.
